Common Questions

Why are you so serious all the time?

Because the conditions we live under are serious. Humor has its place, but it should never replace clear thinking.

Do you hate everything?

No. I hate systems that exploit people while pretending to help them. There’s a difference.

What’s the point of all this analysis?

Understanding how power works is the first step toward changing it. Without that, action is just noise.

Are you trying to start a revolution?

I’m trying to make sure people stop being easily deceived. What they do with that knowledge is up to them.
